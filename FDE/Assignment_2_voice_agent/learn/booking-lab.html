The core safety contract

Tools ground the answer; they do not just decorate it.

  • check_availability requires check-in, check-out, and guest count. It must run before Aurora offers rooms.
  • create_booking requires the selected room, stay details, name, and contact—but the policy also requires an explicit confirmation before calling it.
  • transfer_to_human is the safe exit for a human request, ambiguity, unsupported work, or a risky situation.
  • end_call supplies the control signal that lets voice_loop.py finish a call cleanly.
